# Comprehensive Pakistan Studies Question & Answer Handbook

## Chapter 1: Pre-Independence Era & Founding Fathers

### Q1: When and by whom was the Indian National Congress (INC) established?

**Answer:** The Indian National Congress (INC) was established in **December 1885** in Bombay. It was founded by **Allan Octavian Hume** (a retired British civil servant), along with prominent Indian leaders including Dinshaw Wacha and Dadabhai Naoroji.

### Q2: When and where was the All India Muslim League (AIML) founded, and what were its main objectives?

**Answer:** The All India Muslim League was established on **December 30, 1906**, in **Dhaka** (now in Bangladesh) during the annual meeting of the All India Muhammadan Educational Conference.
Key objectives included:

1. Protecting and advancing the political rights and interests of Indian Muslims.

2. Promoting feelings of loyalty to the British government among Muslims.

3. Preventing the rise of hostility between Muslims and other communities in India.

### Q3: When did Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah complete his legal studies, and where did he study?

**Answer:** Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah traveled to England in 1892 to study law at **Lincoln's Inn** in London. He qualified as a barrister in **1895**, becoming the youngest Indian to be called to the Bar at that time.

### Q4: When did Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah join the All India Muslim League?

**Answer:** Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah joined the All India Muslim League in **1913**, upon the persuasion of Maulana Mohammad Ali Jauhar and Syed Wazir Hasan, while maintaining his existing membership in the Indian National Congress.

### Q5: What were the key biographical dates and educational achievements of Allama Muhammad Iqbal?

**Answer:**

* **Birth:** Allama Muhammad Iqbal was born on **November 9, 1877**, in Sialkot, Punjab.

* **PhD Studies:** Iqbal earned his PhD in Philosophy from the **Ludwig Maximilians University of Munich, Germany**, in **1908**. His doctoral thesis was titled *"The Development of Metaphysics in Persia"*.

* **Poetry & Vision:** Iqbal was a celebrated poet-philosopher in Urdu and Persian. His poetry inspired political and spiritual awakening among Muslims of the subcontinent, earning him the title *Shair-e-Mashriq* (Poet of the East).

* **Death:** Allama Muhammad Iqbal passed away on **April 21, 1938**, in Lahore.

### Q6: What was the significance of the 1946 General Elections for the Pakistan Movement?

**Answer:** The **1945–1946 General Elections** in British India served as a virtual referendum on the demand for Pakistan:

* The All India Muslim League won **100% of the Muslim seats** in the Central Legislative Assembly.

* AIML secured **425 out of 496 reserved Muslim seats** across provincial assemblies (over 86% of the Muslim vote).

* The overwhelming victory proved that the Muslim League was the sole representative body of Indian Muslims and solidified the mandate for the creation of Pakistan.

## Chapter 2: Independence, Early Statehood, & Constitutional History

### Q7: When was Pakistan officially established, and who were its initial top leaders?

**Answer:**

* **Establishment Date:** Pakistan emerged as a sovereign state on **August 14, 1947**.

* **First Governor-General:** **Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah** took the oath as the first Governor-General of Pakistan.

* **First Prime Minister:** **Nawabzada Liaquat Ali Khan** became the first Prime Minister of Pakistan.

### Q8: When did Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah pass away?

**Answer:** Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah passed away on **September 11, 1948**, in Karachi, just thirteen months after the creation of Pakistan.

### Q9: What are the details of the First Constitution of Pakistan (1956)?

**Answer:**

* **Promulgation Date:** March 23, 1956.

* **System of Government:** Parliamentary form of government with a unicameral legislature (National Assembly).

* **Key Features:** Declared Pakistan an "Islamic Republic," introduced parity between East and West Pakistan, and provided for a President as Head of State and Prime Minister as Head of Government. Abrogated in October 1958 due to martial law.

### Q10: What are the details of the Second Constitution of Pakistan (1962)?

**Answer:**

* **Promulgation Date:** June 8, 1962 (enacted under Field Marshal Ayub Khan).

* **System of Government:** Presidential system with executive power concentrated in the President.

* **Key Features:** Unicameral legislature, indirect election system called "Basic Democracies," and equal political representation between East and West Pakistan. Abrogated in March 1969.

### Q11: What are the details of the Third Constitution of Pakistan (1973)?

**Answer:**

* **Passing & Enactment:** Passed unanimously by the Parliament and enacted on **August 14, 1973**.

* **System of Government:** Federal parliamentary system with a bicameral legislature (Senate and National Assembly).

* **Key Features:** Executive authority rests with the Prime Minister. It declares Islam as the state religion and remains the governing constitution of Pakistan today, subject to various amendments.

## Chapter 3: Conflicts, Geopolitics, & National Milestones

### Q12: What was the 1965 Pak-Indo War?

**Answer:** The 1965 Pak-Indo War was an armed conflict between India and Pakistan fought from **September 1 to September 22, 1965**. It began following skirmishes over Kashmir and culminated in major tank battles and aerial combat. The war concluded with a United States/UN-mandated ceasefire and the signing of the **Tashkent Declaration** in January 1966.

### Q13: What caused the 1971 Pak-Indo War and the creation of Bangladesh?

**Answer:** Political tensions, geographical separation, and election outcome disputes following the 1970 elections escalated into military conflict in East Pakistan. Full-scale war broke out between Pakistan and India in December 1971. The conflict concluded on **December 16, 1971**, resulting in the separation of East Pakistan and the creation of the independent nation of **Bangladesh**.

### Q14: When did Pakistan win the ICC Cricket World Cup?

**Answer:** Pakistan won its first ICC Cricket World Cup on **March 25, 1992**, at the Melbourne Cricket Ground in Australia, under the captaincy of **Imran Khan**, defeating England by 22 runs in the final.

### Q15: When and how did Pakistan become a declared nuclear power?

**Answer:** Pakistan became the world's 7th nuclear weapons state and the first in the Muslim world on **May 28, 1998**. Pakistan conducted five successful underground nuclear tests at **Chagai, Balochistan** (Chagai-I), followed by a sixth test on May 30, 1998 (Chagai-II), in response to India's nuclear tests earlier that month.

### Q16: What was the 1999 Pak-Indo War (Kargil Conflict)?

**Answer:** The 1999 conflict was an armed engagement between India and Pakistan in the high-altitude **Kargil district** of Jammu and Kashmir between **May and July 1999**. Fighting ceased following diplomatic intervention, primarily by the United States, prompting a withdrawal to the Line of Control (LoC).

### Q17: What occurred during the February 2019 Pak-Indo aerial engagement (Operation Swift Retort)?

**Answer:** On **February 27, 2019**, following Indian airstrikes in Balakot, the Pakistan Air Force (PAF) conducted counter-strikes across the Line of Control. During the ensuing dogfight, PAF shot down an Indian Air Force MiG-21 Bison fighter jet and captured its pilot, **Wing Commander Abhinandan Varthaman**, who was later released on March 1, 2019, as a peace gesture.

### Q18: What was the outcome of the May 2025 aerial engagement between Pakistan and India?

**Answer:** In **May 2025**, intense aerial clashes erupted between Pakistan and India along the LoC and international border. During the multi-day conflict (*Marka-e-Haq*), the Pakistan Air Force successfully shot down **6 Indian military aircraft** (including modern Rafale jets), establishing air superiority before a US-brokered ceasefire was reached on May 10, 2025.

## Chapter 4: Key Political Figures, Military Leaders, & Scientists

### Q19: Who was Field Marshal Ayub Khan and what were his key contributions and tenure?

**Answer:** 

* **Role:** Field Marshal Muhammad Ayub Khan was Pakistan's second President and the country's first military ruler.

* **Tenure:** He assumed power after imposing Martial Law in **October 1958** and served as President until **March 1969**.

* **Key Achievements & Events:** 

  1. Introduced the **1962 Constitution** based on a presidential system.

  2. Initiated the **Green Revolution** in agriculture and major industrial development projects (including the Indus Waters Treaty of 1960 and construction of Mangla and Tarbela dams).

  3. Led the nation during the **1965 Pak-Indo War**.

### Q20: Who was General Muhammad Zia-ul-Haq and what marked his era of governance?

**Answer:**

* **Role:** General Muhammad Zia-ul-Haq was the Chief of Army Staff who became the 6th President of Pakistan after declaring Martial Law.

* **Tenure:** He ruled from **July 1977 until his death in a plane crash on August 17, 1988**.

* **Key Achievements & Events:**

  1. Implemented a widespread campaign of **Islamization** in legal, economic, and educational systems.

  2. Oversaw Pakistan's strategic involvement in the **Soviet-Afghan War** during the 1980s in alliance with the United States and international partners.

  3. Passed the **8th Amendment** to the Constitution, empowering the President to dissolve the National Assembly.

### Q21: Who was General Pervez Musharraf and what were the significant developments during his rule?

**Answer:**

* **Role:** General Pervez Musharraf was the Chief of Army Staff who took power in a bloodless coup in October 1999 and served as President of Pakistan from **2001 to 2008**.

* **Key Achievements & Events:**

  1. Key commander during the **1999 Kargil Conflict**.

  2. Introduced the concept of **"Enlightened Moderation"** and expanded private media networks in Pakistan.

  3. Aligned Pakistan as a key non-NATO ally of the United States in the global **War on Terror** post-9/11.

  4. Imposed Emergency Rule in November 2007 and resigned as President in August 2008 under threat of impeachment.

### Q22: Who was Dr. Abdul Qadeer Khan and what was his contribution to Pakistan?

**Answer:**

* **Role:** Dr. Abdul Qadeer Khan (Dr. A.Q. Khan) was a world-renowned Pakistani metallurgical engineer and nuclear physicist, widely revered as the **"Father of Pakistan's Atomic Bomb."**

* **Contributions:** 

  1. Founded the **Khan Research Laboratories (KRL)** in Kahuta in 1976.

  2. Successfully developed the uranium enrichment process that allowed Pakistan to build nuclear weapons capability, culminating in the **Chagai nuclear tests of May 1998**.

* **Legacy:** Passed away on **October 10, 2021**, and was given a state funeral with full military honors.

### Q23: Who was Shaheed Benazir Bhutto and what were her historical achievements?

**Answer:**

* **Role:** Benazir Bhutto was the chairperson of the Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) and the daughter of Zulfikar Ali Bhutto.

* **Historical Milestone:** On **December 2, 1988**, she became the **first female Prime Minister** of Pakistan and the first woman elected to lead a Muslim-majority country.

* **Tenures:** Served twice as Prime Minister (1st term: 1988–1990; 2nd term: 1993–1996).

* **Assassination:** She was assassinated in a terrorist attack in Rawalpindi on **December 27, 2007**.

### Q24: Who is Mian Muhammad Nawaz Sharif and what are his major political achievements?

**Answer:**

* **Role:** Mian Muhammad Nawaz Sharif is a veteran politician, leader of the Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz (PML-N), and the longest-serving Prime Minister of Pakistan across non-consecutive terms.

* **Tenures:** Served three terms as Prime Minister of Pakistan (1st: 1990–1993; 2nd: 1997–1999; 3rd: 2013–2017).

* **Key Milestones:**

  1. Authorized Pakistan's nuclear weapons tests in **May 1998** (*Youm-e-Takbeer*).

  2. Spearheaded major infrastructure projects, including Pakistan's Motorway network (M-2) and the **China-Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC)** launch in 2015.

### Q25: Who is Imran Ahmad Khan Niazi and what are his notable career milestones?

**Answer:**

* **Role:** Imran Khan is a former international cricket icon, philanthropist, and founder/chairman of Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI).

* **Sports & Charity:** Captained Pakistan to victory in the **1992 ICC Cricket World Cup** and established Shaukat Khanum Memorial Cancer Hospital & Research Centre.

* **Political Tenure:** Served as the **22nd Prime Minister of Pakistan** from **August 2018 to April 2022**.

* **Key Events:** Oversaw the diplomatic and military response during the **2019 Pak-Indo standoff (Operation Swift Retort)** and launched social welfare programs like *Ehsaas*.

### Q26: Who is Asif Ali Zardari and what are his political contributions?

**Answer:**

* **Role:** Asif Ali Zardari is the Co-Chairman of the Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) and prominent statesman.

* **Presidential Tenures:** Served as the **11th President of Pakistan** from **2008 to 2013**, becoming the first democratic president in Pakistan's history to complete a full 5-year term. Was re-elected as the **14th President of Pakistan in March 2024**.

* **Key Contributions:** Surrendered key presidential powers to Parliament through the landmark **18th Constitutional Amendment (2010)**, restoring the Parliamentary democratic character of the Constitution.

### Q27: Who is Bilawal Bhutto Zardari and what roles has he served in Pakistani politics?

**Answer:**

* **Role:** Bilawal Bhutto Zardari is the Chairman of the Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) and son of Shaheed Benazir Bhutto and Asif Ali Zardari.

* **Key Positions:**

  1. Appointed Chairman of the PPP in December 2007 following his mother's assassination.

  2. Served as the **Foreign Minister of Pakistan** from April 2022 to August 2023, representing Pakistan in key international forums and strengthening bilateral relations globally.

### Q28: Who is Maryam Nawaz Sharif and what is her historic political achievement?

**Answer:**

* **Role:** Maryam Nawaz Sharif is a senior political leader and Senior Vice President of the Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz (PML-N), as well as the daughter of Mian Muhammad Nawaz Sharif.

* **Historic Milestone:** On **February 26, 2024**, she took the oath as the Chief Minister of Punjab, becoming the **first female Chief Minister** of any province in the history of Pakistan.
